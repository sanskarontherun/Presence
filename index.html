Why présence

Présence began with a feeling difficult to name — the quiet ache of seeing so much beauty pass through the world without leaving a trace.

Sanskar is drawn to that fleeting instant when an image, a word, a shadow of light suddenly becomes more than itself, when the ordinary opens a small door into somewhere unknown.

For him, a website is not a frame around a person's work; it is the room their imagination enters, the light beneath the door, the silence between two thoughts.

He builds not to fill the screen, but to give the unseen somewhere to breathe. Every line, every space, every movement is placed with intention, as though the page were being remembered rather than designed.

Perhaps that is what Présence seeks: to take something that exists only within a person, and give it a quiet place in the world.
